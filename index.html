How to reach

Air Force Station Palam sits in Delhi Cantonment, in south-west Delhi, between the airport and Dhaula Kuan.

  • By air

    Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL)

    The station adjoins the airport; the drive from either terminal is roughly 15 to 25 minutes depending on traffic. Prepaid taxis and app cabs are available at both terminals.

  • By train

    Delhi Cantt railway station

    The closest railhead, a short drive from the venue. New Delhi railway station is about 45 to 60 minutes away by road; Hazrat Nizamuddin about the same.

  • By metro

    Delhi Cantt (Pink Line) or Palam (Magenta Line)

    Take an auto or cab from the station to the venue. Share the pinned location with the driver so you are dropped at the correct gate.

  • By road

    Driving in

    Approach via Dhaula Kuan or the NH-48 airport stretch, then follow the pinned location to the venue gate. Valet service is available at the venue.

If you need any conveyance, please let us know on the numbers given below and we will gladly arrange a suitable mode of transport for you.

Before you come

A few things that make entry into the station smooth for everyone.

  1. Carry a government photo ID

    Aadhaar, driving licence, passport or voter ID. Every adult guest is checked at the gate; please keep the original handy, not a photocopy or a photo on your phone.

  2. Valet service

    Valet parking will be available at the venue, so drive right up to the gate and hand over your keys.

  3. Keep the traffic in mind

    Evening traffic around Dhaula Kuan and the airport stretch can be slow, so prior arrival is advised. Leave a little earlier than you think you need to.

  4. Photography

    Photos and videos are welcome at the celebration. Please do not photograph the gate, the airfield or any station area on the way in.

  5. Dress code

    It's a night to celebrate! Dress to look the happiest.

  6. Staying over

    Anyone wishing to stay the night is requested to share their requirements in advance, so that adequate arrangements can be made.
